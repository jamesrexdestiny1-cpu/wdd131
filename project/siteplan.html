Culture, Heritage and Destinations

Site Name

Discover Nigeria

Discover Nigeria represents a website dedicated to showcasing the country's rich cultural heritage, traditions, local foods, festivals and tourist attractions. The name was chosen because it is simple, memorable and communicates the purpose of helping visitors explore and appreciate Nigeria.

Optional domain: discovernigeria.org

Logo: The DN logo represents Discover Nigeria and will appear in the website header to support brand recognition.

Site Purpose

The purpose of Discover Nigeria is to provide an informative and engaging platform where Nigerians and international visitors can explore Nigeria's cultural heritage, ethnic diversity, traditional festivals, local cuisines and tourist attractions. The website will present useful information, photographs and dedicated pages covering different aspects of Nigerian culture. It will also provide an interactive visitor feedback form through which users can share their experiences, opinions and suggestions.

Scenarios

These questions represent the information visitors may seek when using the website.

  • What are the major ethnic groups in Nigeria, and what cultural practices distinguish them?
  • Which traditional festivals are celebrated in different parts of Nigeria?
  • What are some popular Nigerian traditional foods, and where do they originate?
  • What tourist attractions and historical sites can I visit in Nigeria?
  • How can I share my feedback or suggestions about this website?

Typography

Two Google Fonts have been selected to create a consistent, readable and attractive appearance throughout the website.

Poppins

Usage: Website title, navigation, section headings and prominent titles.

Open Sans

Usage: Paragraphs, descriptions, form labels and general website content.
